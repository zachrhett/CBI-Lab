Productive Attitudes, Behaviors & Mental Paradigms

Educational catalog of traits, practices, and frameworks associated with more favorable personal and social outcomes under demand — especially keeping stress adaptive rather than erosive. Not scored in the calculator.

These are resources and ideals for reflection, not requirements or moral rankings. Context, culture, disability, and safety shape what is possible. Skills can be learned; none erase real structural load by themselves.
