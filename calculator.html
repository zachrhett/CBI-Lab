Interactive Cognitive Burden Index

Expanded exploratory model: demand factors, recovery, optional Big Five sensitivity, and dynamic accumulation. Hypothetical personal tool — not diagnostic. See Mathematics for equations.

Purpose: identify factors that turn neutral stress into erosive / toxic load when prolonged and unchecked — so earlier signals and recovery strategies can prevent long-term harm. Stress is not the enemy; missing recovery, agency, and support is. Hypothetical personal model — not diagnosis.

From signal to strategy

When the index or timeline drifts into erosive risk, the model points at levers — not labels.

Protect recovery

Sleep, detachment, micro-breaks, and protected off-time convert demand back toward adaptive stress. Recovery opportunity is the strongest counterweight to latent accumulation.

Restore controllability

Unclear priorities, zero influence over pace, and helplessness appraisals turn the same workload toxic. Clarifying agency is a primary prevention move.

Cut extraneous demand

Interruptions, switching, and display-rule intensity often add load without adding value. Mental ergonomics targets these first.

Support the body & context

Alcohol/substance load, diet instability, environmental harshness, and unresolved money/resource strain all lower the threshold at which work stress becomes erosive.
