Interactive Cognitive Burden Index

Expanded exploratory model: demand factors, recovery, optional Big Five sensitivity, and dynamic accumulation. Hypothetical personal tool — not diagnostic. See Mathematics for equations.

Personality sliders only retune relative weights (how costly different demands feel in this toy model). They do not score health, morality, or ability.

Demand & context

How often attention must reorient between distinct tasks or contexts.
Volume and complexity of information held and manipulated.
Constraint on available time relative to required work.
Frequency and disruptiveness of external interruptions.
Affective demand, conflict, or sustained self-regulation.
How little influence the person has over pace, priorities, or outcomes.
Recent sleep loss, fragmentation, or circadian disruption.
Absence of helpful colleagues, supervisors, or personal support.
Protected rest, detachment, and restorative time (higher is better).

Big Five sensitivity optional

Scales 0–1. Midpoint ≈ average. Affects weight distribution only.

Slightly buffers task-switching weight in this model when higher.
Slightly reduces interruption weight when higher.
Raises relative weight of emotional load, sleep, low control, and recovery deficit.
