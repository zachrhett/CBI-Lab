References & studies cited

Integrity layer for this personal lab. These works inform the conceptual framing. CBI itself is not a validated instrument derived from these papers — it is a transparent heuristic synthesis for exploration and education.

Citations are representative teaching references, not an exhaustive systematic review. Prefer primary sources when writing or deciding anything consequential.

Allostasis & allostatic load

  • Sterling, P., & Eyer, J. (1988). Allostasis: A new paradigm to explain arousal pathology. In S. Fisher & J. Reason (Eds.), Handbook of life stress, cognition and health. Wiley.
  • McEwen, B. S. (1998). Protective and damaging effects of stress mediators. New England Journal of Medicine, 338(3), 171–179.
  • McEwen, B. S. (2000). Allostasis and allostatic load: Implications for neuropsychopharmacology. Neuropsychopharmacology, 22(2), 108–124.
  • McEwen, B. S., & Wingfield, J. C. (2003). The concept of allostasis in biology and biomedicine. Hormones and Behavior, 43(1), 2–15.
  • Seeman, T. E., Singer, B. H., Rowe, J. W., Horwitz, R. I., & McEwen, B. S. (1997). Price of adaptation—allostatic load and its health consequences. Archives of Internal Medicine, 157(19), 2259–2268.
  • Juster, R.-P., McEwen, B. S., & Lupien, S. J. (2010). Allostatic load biomarkers of chronic stress and impact on health and cognition. Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews, 35(1), 2–16.

Cognitive load & instructional / task design

  • Sweller, J. (1988). Cognitive load during problem solving: Effects on learning. Cognitive Science, 12(2), 257–285.
  • Sweller, J., van Merrienboer, J. J. G., & Paas, F. (1998). Cognitive architecture and instructional design. Educational Psychology Review, 10(3), 251–296.

Mental workload & human factors

  • Hart, S. G., & Staveland, L. E. (1988). Development of NASA-TLX (Task Load Index). In P. A. Hancock & N. Meshkati (Eds.), Human mental workload. North-Holland.

  • Wickens, C. D., Hollands, J. G., Banbury, S., & Parasuraman, R. (2015). Engineering psychology and human performance (4th ed.). Psychology Press.

  • ISO 6385 (various editions). Ergonomics principles in the design of work systems. International Organization for Standardization.

  • Meijman, T. F., & Mulder, G. (1998). Psychological aspects of workload. In P. J. D. Drenth et al. (Eds.), Handbook of work and organizational psychology. Psychology Press.
  • Sonnentag, S., & Fritz, C. (2007). The Recovery Experience Questionnaire. Journal of Occupational Health Psychology, 12(3), 204–221.
  • World Health Organization. (2019/2022 contexts). Burnout as an occupational phenomenon in ICD-11 framing (see WHO materials on burnout).

Stress, controllability, and cognition (selected)

  • Lupien, S. J., McEwen, B. S., Gunnar, M. R., & Heim, C. (2009). Effects of stress throughout the lifespan on the brain, behaviour and cognition. Nature Reviews Neuroscience, 10(6), 434–445.

  • Sapolsky, R. M. (2004). Why zebras don’t get ulcers (3rd ed.). Holt. (Accessible synthesis; not a primary methods paper.)

About this lab’s use of sources

Factor weights, the γ amplification term, and the latent-\(L\) recursion are heuristic choices for a personal model. They are ordered in a way meant to be consistent with the literatures above, not fitted to a dataset. Do not treat CBI scores as clinical cutoffs or as measurements equivalent to NASA-TLX, allostatic-load biomarker batteries, or diagnostic criteria.
