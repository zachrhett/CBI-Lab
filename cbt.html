CBT Skills Library

Educational catalog of Cognitive Behavioral Therapy skills that support keeping stress adaptive rather than erosive. Not therapy, not scored in the calculator.

CBT is one of the best-studied psychotherapeutic approaches. This page teaches concepts for prevention literacy. It does not replace a qualified clinician, especially for severe depression, trauma, psychosis, or crisis (US: 988).
