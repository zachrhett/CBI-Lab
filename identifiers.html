Archive of Identifiers

A structured catalog of markers and constructs used when studying cognitive burden, allostatic strain, and related psychological correlates. Identifiers are for observation and research framing — not for diagnosis.

How to read this archive. Each entry is a named construct or observable pattern. “Related CBI factors” points to components of the interactive model that often move with that identifier. Correlation is not causation; many identifiers are bidirectional with load.
