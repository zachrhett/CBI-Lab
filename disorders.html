Mental Disorders Reference

Educational catalog of major categories used in clinical language (including historical “neurosis” terms). Not diagnostic. Not scored in the calculator. Prolonged toxic stress can correlate with risk or worsening of some presentations; correlation is not destiny.

Educational only. Descriptions are simplified summaries for learning and systems thinking. Only a qualified clinician can diagnose. If you or someone else is in crisis, contact local emergency services or a crisis line (e.g. 988 in the US).
